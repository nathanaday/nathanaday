
Hi, I'm Nathan! 

### Professional experience

During my day job, I perform R&D for our awesome, cloud-based [fire detection products](https://movitherm.com/itl/). I've been working on this solution since its inception back in 2021, and to date we have stopped quite a few fires in our clients' facilities. I design and deploy on all levels of the stack, from our Yocto Linux embedded systems, to computer vision training in infrared, and lately, lots of AWS service orchestration.

### Graduate research

I'm also a second year MSCS student at USC. My current course load focuses on applications for [autonomous vehicles](https://github.com/nathanaday/autonomous-vehicle-sandbox) from two perspectives: 
- 🚗 cyber-physical control - building a high level planner to route autonomous vehicles safely through unknown situations 
- 🚙 [deep learning perception](https://github.com/kgqiao/monocular-depth-confidence-estimation) - adding a trained confidence head to Depth Anything
